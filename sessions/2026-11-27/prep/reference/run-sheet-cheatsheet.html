2 · Grid: 23 points, 10–100 kHz (≈ 45–60 min)

python3 supervisor_script.py sessions/2026-11-27/base-config_B_grid.json
python3 calculations/loss_breakdown.py sessions/2026-11-27/configs/B_grid -o sessions/2026-11-27/B_grid_breakdown.csv
  • < 30 kHz: small phases (2.4–45°). This firmware handles them (±0.05°).
  • Highest phase: 168° at 100 kHz / 3.5 A (limit 175°).
  • Capacitor resonance (electrolytic, 125 mΩ ESR): write down the frequencies where the waveforms or P_remaining jump. Exclude them later, not now.
  • Runs follow os.walk order, not sorted by frequency. That's expected.

3 · Optional: dead-time compensation A/B

Copy the anchors group under a new Experiment_name (e.g. A_anchors_dtc) with "DeadTimeComp": 1, regenerate, and run it. The duty becomes D − td·f/2 (0.485 at 100 kHz). Compare the mean of iL on the scope and P_remaining against the plain anchors.

A group won't run twice (base-config-original.json guard). To rerun, change Experiment_name and regenerate.

4 · When to stop, and the fallback

Stop if: a write is refused (ThingSetShieldError in the log) · the scope trigger is missing · the gate signals don't match the commanded phase.
Stopping mid-run: Ctrl-C, then switch the supply output off on its front panel. The SPIN stops its legs on IDLE/POWER_OFF.

Fallback: flash owntech/spin_code_compiled.mcuboot.bin and set "shield_protocol": "string", "shield_pid": 257 in the configs.
Limits: whole degrees only and nothing below 50 kHz. Only the anchors and the grid points ≥ 55 kHz can run.
